Strategic Dynamics
Vertical Integration
Chipmaking moves in-house
Apple, Google, Microsoft, and Amazon have all moved into custom silicon design, bypassing merchant chip vendors to capture margin and optimize for proprietary workloads. This blurs the semiconductor and hardware stages.
Geopolitical Risk
Fab concentration creates exposure
Over 90% of leading-edge logic fabrication is concentrated in Taiwan (TSMC) and South Korea (Samsung). Export controls on advanced chips and ASML equipment have made semiconductor supply chains a central geopolitical lever.
AI Value Shift
Gravity shifting toward inference
AI model training concentrates value in GPU-rich cloud providers and frontier labs. As inference scales, value may migrate toward application-layer companies able to embed AI into domain-specific workflows at marginal cost.
Platform Power
Middleware as a moat
Cloud platforms and app stores act as toll roads between hardware and software. AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and the mobile duopoly (Apple + Google) capture significant margin while developers and ISVs compete on their surfaces.
