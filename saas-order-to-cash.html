Order-to-Cash Process Flow
1

Lead & Qualification

Prospect identified and qualification criteria met. Lead entered into system with company profile, use case, and budget estimate.

2

Demo & Evaluation

Product demonstration and trial access provided. Prospect evaluates solution fit. Technical and business stakeholders engaged.

3

Quote & Proposal

Customized pricing proposal generated based on edition, term (monthly/annual), add-ons, and volume discounts. Terms and conditions documented.

4

Contract Negotiation

Legal review, contract modifications, and stakeholder approvals. Signature authority validated. Digital signature collected.

5

Order & Invoice

Sales order created. Invoice generated for initial term. Payment terms defined (net 30, net 60, etc.). Invoice sent to customer.

6

Fulfillment & Onboarding

Customer account provisioned. Seats/users activated. Onboarding resources provided. Training session scheduled. Success manager assigned.

7

Payment Collection

Payment collected via credit card, ACH, or wire transfer. Payment confirmation recorded. Revenue recognized according to ASC 606.

8

Renewal & Expansion

Renewal notice sent 90 days before expiration. Expansion opportunities identified. Upsell/cross-sell presented. Renewal negotiated and closed.

💡 This interactive tool helps strategy practitioners visualize and optimize the Order-to-Cash process for SaaS businesses.
